How the system works

A simple view of the monitoring pipeline behind the dashboards.

1. Open sourcesOfficial statements, reporting, social and geospatial sources.
2. Automated collectionScheduled workflows collect and update structured source data.
3. ClassificationEvents are normalized, categorized and geolocated where possible.
4. Historical storageStructured datasets preserve events for temporal comparison.
5. AnalysisMaps and specialist dashboards expose patterns and analytical signals.

Methodology & interpretation

What this platform is for

The monitor supports OSINT-oriented situational awareness and exploratory analysis. Different dashboards use different source sets and therefore answer different questions. Counts should be interpreted within the methodology of the selected module rather than as a universal measure of conflict intensity.

Source warning

Official-source datasets represent statements made by the issuing organization. They do not independently verify reported strikes, targets, casualties or claimed operational outcomes.
